Every question hides a strange-but-true answer. Write a fake one so good your friends pick it, and find the real one yourself.

Room mode isn't switched on yet. It needs the Supabase address in config.js.

How to play
  1. A strange-but-true question appears with a blank.
  2. Everyone secretly writes a fake answer that sounds real.
  3. All the fakes are mixed in with the real answer.
  4. Everyone picks the one they think is true.
  5. +1000 for finding the truth, +500 for every player you fool. The last round is worth double.

This device: pass one phone or tablet around. Room: a TV or laptop shows the game and everyone plays on their own phone.
